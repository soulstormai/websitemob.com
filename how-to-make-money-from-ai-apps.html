AdSense, affiliate links, Stripe subscriptions — the four income streams that actually convert when your users arrive. Real numbers, real strategies, no fluff.

The easy part is building the app. Vibe coding tools like Lovable and Base44 get you to a working product fast. The harder question — the one most tutorials skip — is how you actually turn that product into money.

There are four proven income streams for AI-built apps. They're not equal. Some work immediately, some take months to compound. Know which is which before you start building, and design your app with monetization in mind from day one.

Stream 1 — Display Advertising (Google AdSense)

The lowest-effort income stream. Once your site is approved by Google AdSense, ads appear automatically and you earn money based on impressions and clicks. No selling, no customer service, no subscriptions to manage.

The catch: you need traffic. AdSense pays between $2–$15 CPM (per 1,000 views) depending on your niche and audience geography. A calculator tool getting 10,000 monthly visitors might earn $50–$150/month. Not life-changing — but it's passive and it compounds as traffic grows.

Best for: Free tools, calculators, reference apps, blogs. Any app where users arrive from search and consume content.

Setup: Apply at adsense.google.com. Google reviews your site — typically takes 2–4 weeks. Once approved, drop the AdSense script in your HTML and place ad units where they don't break the user experience: between content sections, in sidebars, at the bottom of tools.

Stream 2 — Affiliate Links

You recommend a tool or product. Someone buys or signs up. You earn a commission. No inventory, no customer support, no fulfilment. This is the highest-leverage income stream for apps built in the vibe coding space because your audience is actively looking for tools — and the commissions are real.

The key is relevance. An affiliate link to Shopify in a tip calculator converts poorly. An affiliate link to Shopify in an article about starting an online business converts very well. Place affiliate recommendations where intent is highest — at the moment your user has a problem and you're about to introduce the solution.

Stream 3 — Stripe Freemium

Free tier does the job. Paid tier removes limits or adds power features. This is the SaaS model applied to apps you built in an afternoon.

The economics work best when your free tier is genuinely useful but has a clear ceiling. A tip calculator is free. A tip calculator with multi-currency support, export to PDF, saved calculations, and team features is $3.99/month. Most users stay free — that's fine. Even a 3% conversion rate on 1,000 monthly active users is 30 paying customers at $3.99 = $120/month recurring, growing every month.

Setup with Lovable or Base44: Both have Stripe integration built in. Describe the freemium structure to the AI and it handles the payment flow, subscription management, and feature gating. You don't write billing code.

Stream 4 — Email List + Product Sales

The only audience you actually own. Social media can deplatform you. Search rankings can drop. Your email list can't be taken away.

Every app should capture emails. A simple "get weekly tips" offer at the bottom of your tool, connected to a free email service like Mailchimp or ConvertKit, builds an asset that compounds over time. With a list of 1,000 engaged subscribers, you can sell a $29 guide, a $99 course, or a $199 toolkit — and make more in a single email than a month of AdSense revenue.

The list also amplifies every other income stream. New affiliate deal? Email your list. New app launched? Email your list. New article that happens to include your Shopify affiliate link? Email your list.

The Right Order to Build Them

Month 1: Apply for AdSense. Get approved. Drop ad units on everything you publish. Low revenue now, growing over time.

Month 1–2: Sign up for affiliate programs. Add relevant links naturally to every piece of content. Apply for Lovable's program if you're writing about AI tools.

Month 2–3: Add email capture to every app and article. Start sending a weekly newsletter. Build the list before you need it.

Month 3+: Add Stripe to your most-used app. Keep the free tier generous. Make the paid tier genuinely worth paying for.

None of these are fast. All of them compound. The apps you build with AI this month could be paying you passively in 12 months. That's the actual opportunity — not the build, but the long game after it.

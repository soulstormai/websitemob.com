Stop giving clients your personal number. The best business phone systems for small teams and solo founders — compared by price, features, and what actually matters when a customer calls.

// BY WEBSITEMOB// MAY 2026// 5 MIN READ

If you're running any kind of business and still using your personal number for client calls, you're creating problems that will cost you later: no call routing when you're busy, no professional greeting, no way to separate work calls from personal calls, no voicemail transcription, no call logs for your CRM.

A business phone system solves all of that. And in 2026, the good ones cost less than a dinner out. Here's what to look for and which platforms are worth your money.

What You Actually Need

For most small businesses and solo founders, the essentials are: a dedicated business number, a professional auto-attendant greeting, voicemail to email, call forwarding to your mobile, and basic call logs. Everything beyond that — video conferencing, SMS campaigns, CRM integrations, team inboxes — is useful but not urgent on day one.

The mistake most people make is paying for an enterprise system when they need a solo plan. The second mistake is going too cheap and ending up with a number that can't forward calls, drops audio quality, or doesn't have a mobile app.

Alternatives Worth Considering

Google Voice (Free, Minimal)

Free with a Google account. Gives you a business number that forwards to your personal number, basic voicemail transcription, and a simple mobile app. Works well for solo founders who just need a separate number and nothing else. No auto-attendant, no team features, no CRM integration. If you outgrow it, migrating is painless.

OpenPhone ($15/mo)

A modern, well-designed business phone app popular with startups. Cleaner interface than Nextiva, shared team inboxes, good SMS features, and direct integration with HubSpot and Slack. Better for tech teams who want a slick product. Less enterprise depth than Nextiva.

RingCentral ($30/mo+)

Enterprise-grade system with every feature you'll ever need — and the pricing to match. Best for teams of 10+ who need advanced call routing, conference bridges, and compliance features. Overkill for solo founders and small teams.

The One Thing That Matters Most

Whatever you pick, set up a professional voicemail greeting on day one. "Hi, you've reached [your name] at [business]. I'll return your call within one business day. Please leave a message." That alone, delivered professionally, changes how potential clients perceive your business before they've even spoken to you.

Your phone system is often the first impression. Make it count.
